## Audit Interpretation

- The legacy PPO baseline has enough complete artifacts to support infrastructure/audit use.
- Complete artifact availability does not prove trading edge.
- Benchmark evidence is mixed because several complete artifacts underperform buy-and-hold.
- Cash-baseline evidence alone is not enough for promotion because a trading model must clear buy-and-hold, cost, stability, and leakage standards.
- Prior no-submit evidence showed recurring candidates, especially AMD and UNH, but candidate-set stability was not established.
- Multi-order instability remains a blocker for controlled submit.
- Leakage, embargo, transaction-cost, and turnover sufficiency are not fully verified by this local artifact audit alone.
