RE-AUDIT RESULT: PASS

## Blocking findings

None.

The corrected draft fully resolves the four previously blocking categories. It now uses role-specific governance anchors, removes tracker-like fields, defines a complete multi-part activation condition, expressly prohibits model-artifact and downstream activity, and requires the full authorization provenance chain in the later research record.

## Non-blocking observations

* The governance anchors now correctly distinguish the planning authorization record, planning record, and planning review record.
* No `latest_completed_*` or equivalent current-checkpoint-tracker fields remain.
* Authorization effectiveness is expressly conditioned on all of the following:

  * independent review PASS;
  * authorization record committed and pushed;
  * review record committed and pushed;
  * explicit `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` acceptance committed and pushed.
* The draft expressly states that independent review PASS alone does not authorize execution and that commit or push alone does not authorize execution.
* `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` is correctly identified as the controlling source of truth for the active broad state and authorization boundaries.
* The milestone and future-validation reference maps are correctly limited to navigation and guidance functions and are not treated as authorization sources or current checkpoint trackers.
* The authorized scope is limited to a later, separately executed review of publicly accessible official documents.
* The draft clearly states that creating or reviewing the authorization record does not itself perform or authorize the research.
* Authenticated account access, account inspection, billing inspection, subscription inspection, entitlement inspection, permission inspection, account changes, subscription changes, purchases, upgrades, downgrades, payment submission, and acceptance of new contractual terms remain prohibited.
* Support contact, form submission, ticket submission, credential activity, secret access, client creation, API calls, provider testing, and technical testing remain prohibited.
* SIP, IEX, and all other market-data access remain prohibited.
* Raw-data, candidate-data, dataset, validation, validation-only preflight, training, retraining, holdout, candidate-selection, model-artifact, order, paper-trading, deployment, release, and tagging activity remain prohibited.
* The catch-all prohibition now explicitly covers model-artifact creation, access, inspection, loading, modification, selection, acceptance, export, publication, and execution as applicable.
* The separate “No implied downstream authorization” section prevents research completion from being interpreted as authorization for any subsequent account, subscription, API, data, validation, model, trading, deployment, release, or tagging action.
* The required research-record provenance includes:

  * the effective authorization record and commit;
  * the independent PASS review record and commit;
  * the pushed `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` acceptance commit.
* The wording is internally consistent and does not imply authorization beyond the narrowly bounded public-document research task.

The statement that independent review is required “before commit” is consistent with the record’s draft-review lifecycle. It does not conflict with the effectiveness requirements, because execution remains prohibited until the authorization record, PASS review, and explicit `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` acceptance have all been committed and pushed.
