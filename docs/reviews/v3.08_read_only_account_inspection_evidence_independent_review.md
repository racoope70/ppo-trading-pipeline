## 7. Findings requiring correction

### Finding 1 — account observations and inspection sequencing lack explicit user-reported provenance

The affirmative account values in sections 4 and 5, the “established” wording in sections 4 and 10, and `effectiveness_condition_satisfied_before_inspection = YES` do not explicitly distinguish user-reported direct observation from independent or provider verification. Add an unambiguous global provenance classification, or qualify each applicable value and conclusion as `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED_USER_REPORTED`. Also classify the pre-inspection effectiveness sequence as user-reported or add sufficient text-only timing evidence. Do not characterize these statements as independently provider-verified.

### Finding 2 — cookie-action field is ambiguous

`cookie_notice_accept_clicked = NO_USER_REPORTED` does not unambiguously establish whether the user reported no click or whether no report was received. Use `NOT_ESTABLISHED` unless an explicit user report supports `NO_DIRECTLY_OBSERVED_USER_REPORTED` or an equally clear classification.

Both findings are correctable documentary issues. They do not establish that the underlying account observations are false, that sensitive information was retained, or that an uncontained material state change occurred.
