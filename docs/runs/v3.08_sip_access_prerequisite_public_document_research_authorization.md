## Background

The PPO v2 superseding dataset reconstruction workstream is blocked because the governed IEX raw dataset is missing 66 required hourly observations.

SIP has not been technically tested as an alternative source because the project has not yet established the applicable:

* entitlement requirements;
* account or plan prerequisites;
* subscription requirements;
* permissions;
* licensing restrictions;
* permitted-use restrictions;
* redistribution restrictions;
* plan-tier requirements;
* pricing or cost requirements;
* historical-data availability conditions.

The project must resolve those prerequisites through a separately controlled public-document research task before considering any account inspection, subscription decision, API activity, provider testing, or market-data access.

## Authorization decision proposed by this draft

Subject to an independent review pass and explicit pushed governance acceptance, this record proposes authorization for one narrowly bounded task:

```text
proposed_authorization_boundary = PUBLIC_DOCUMENT_RESEARCH_ONLY
proposed_authorized_execution_scope = SIP_ACCESS_PREREQUISITE_OFFICIAL_PUBLIC_DOCUMENT_RESEARCH
```

The proposed authorization is limited to collecting and evaluating publicly accessible official documentation needed to determine whether a later SIP technical-access checkpoint could be responsibly proposed.

It does not authorize SIP technical testing or any action required to obtain SIP access.

This authorization can become effective only when the authorization record and its independent PASS review are committed and pushed and a pushed update to `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` explicitly changes the controlling authorization boundary to `PUBLIC_DOCUMENT_RESEARCH_ONLY` with the corresponding narrowly bounded execution scope. No subordinate run, review, audit, milestone-map, or future-reference-map record can independently activate the authorization.

## Authorized research questions

The later research task may address only the following questions:

1. What publicly documented Alpaca market-data plans or service tiers provide SIP access?
2. What publicly documented subscription or payment requirements apply to SIP access?
3. What publicly documented account, geographic, professional-status, or eligibility requirements apply?
4. What publicly documented permissions or entitlements are required?
5. What publicly documented licensing, permitted-use, display, storage, derived-data, or redistribution restrictions apply?
6. What publicly documented historical-data limits, lookback limits, or data-availability conditions apply?
7. What publicly documented rate limits or request restrictions would affect a later technical feasibility assessment?
8. What publicly documented pricing, recurring cost, one-time cost, or usage-based cost information applies?
9. What material prerequisite questions remain unresolved after reviewing the authorized public documents?
10. Whether the available public evidence is sufficient to propose a separate later authorization checkpoint for account-specific inspection or technical SIP testing.

## Authorized source scope

The later research task may inspect only publicly accessible official documents, including publicly accessible:

* Alpaca documentation;
* Alpaca market-data documentation;
* Alpaca pricing or plan-comparison pages;
* Alpaca terms, agreements, policies, and licensing materials;
* Alpaca support or help-center articles that do not require authentication;
* official exchange, regulator, or market-data licensing documents when directly necessary to interpret SIP access or permitted use.

No authenticated page, customer portal, billing portal, account dashboard, private support exchange, or account-specific document is authorized.

## Authorized research outputs

The later research task may produce documentation-only records containing:
