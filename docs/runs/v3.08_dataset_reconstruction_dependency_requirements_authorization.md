4. Session opens and closes are timezone-aware UTC.
5. Representative holidays and non-sessions are excluded correctly.
6. Representative early closes are correct.
7. Whole-interval containment produces the governed full-session and early-close slot counts.
8. Repeated schedule construction is deterministic.
9. Relevant installed and transitive dependency versions are recorded.

These checks are requirements for the future checkpoint, not results of this authorization record.
