## 7. Decision rationale

The completed research already identifies the exact account-specific facts that remain unresolved:

- exact Alpaca plan label;
- account type;
- professional or nonprofessional classification;
- geographic or account eligibility;
- delayed historical SIP entitlement;
- account-specific historical-data rate-limit entitlement;
- account-visible billing or subscription state.

These facts cannot be resolved through more general public documentation because they depend on the user's actual account configuration.

Accordingly, additional public-source research is not necessary to choose the next governance proposal. The appropriate next proposal is a separately bounded read-only account-inspection authorization consideration.

This decision does not conclude that account inspection will resolve every licensing, permitted-use, storage, non-display, derived-data, publication, redistribution, reporting, or fee question. Those questions must be resolved or explicitly governed before any technical SIP-access authorization is proposed.

## 9. Proposed later account-inspection authorization scope

A later authorization proposal may consider permitting read-only inspection of:

- current plan name or tier;
- account type;
- professional-status or market-data classification;
- geographic or eligibility information displayed by the provider;
- delayed historical SIP entitlement;
- account-specific historical-data rate limit;
- existing billing or subscription state;
- account-visible terms already governing the current account, only if separately and explicitly included.
