## PPO-Only Evidence Gate Before Hybrid Work

PPO-only evidence must come before hybrid work.

The next phase should define the PPO observation protocol before considering Random Forest or XGBoost gates. That protocol should specify the observation window, required artifacts, metrics, pass/fail criteria, review cadence, and escalation rules.

The PPO-only evidence gate should require review of:

```text
historical walk-forward validation
untouched holdout validation
leakage controls
train-only preprocessing and normalization controls
fresh supervised paper-trading observation
multi-session decision-state behavior
candidate persistence
paper-trading P&L and drawdown
turnover and trade frequency
changed-candidate and multi-order-plan frequency
broker-state observations when broker review is explicitly required
```

Until that evidence exists, PPO + Random Forest and PPO + XGBoost remain later comparison paths, not active readiness paths.
