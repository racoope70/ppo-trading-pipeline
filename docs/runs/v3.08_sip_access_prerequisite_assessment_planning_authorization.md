The future planning record may define only:

- the exact prerequisite questions concerning SIP entitlement;
- required account or user permissions;
- subscription and plan-tier questions;
- licensing constraints;
- permitted-use restrictions;
- cost and pricing questions;
- authoritative evidence-source categories to be consulted later;
- evidence-capture fields and provenance requirements;
- separation between public-document research, account inspection, account changes, purchases, API calls, and provider-coverage testing;
- result classifications;
- fail-closed criteria;
- stop conditions; and
- the later authorization chain required before each assessment action.

The future planning record may name categories of authoritative sources but may not consult them during the planning checkpoint unless a later authorization expressly permits that research.

## 6. Prohibited scope of the future planning record

The future planning record must not:

- perform public-document or internet research;
- state current plan tiers, prices, subscription terms, licensing terms, or entitlement facts unless already established by committed evidence;
- inspect an account;
- inspect account entitlements or permissions;
- change an account or subscription;
- purchase or upgrade anything;
- create a client;
- call an API;
- request SIP or IEX data;
- test provider coverage;
- recover observations;
- create candidate data;
- alter the reconstruction contract; or
- authorize dataset or downstream execution.
