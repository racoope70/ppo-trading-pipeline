## Required stop conditions

Research must stop at the affected question when:

1. login or authentication is required;
2. account-specific information is required;
3. a subscription or purchase workflow is encountered;
4. acceptance of terms is required;
5. contacting support is required;
6. an API call or client configuration is required;
7. market-data retrieval is required;
8. the question cannot be answered from authorized public documents;
9. a materially different legal, licensing, cost, or governance issue is identified.

A stop condition must be recorded as unresolved and must not be bypassed.

For each authorized source:

1. Confirm that the publisher is an authorized official source.
2. Confirm that the page is publicly accessible without login.
3. Record the exact page title, publisher, public URL, and access date.
4. Identify which authorized research questions the source supports.
5. Record a concise paraphrase of the relevant evidence.
6. Use only a short direct quotation when exact wording is materially important.
7. Distinguish confirmed facts from interpretations.
8. Record limitations and uncertainty.
9. Do not infer historical availability from real-time availability.
10. Do not infer account-specific entitlement from general plan documentation.
