## 6. Why this path was selected

The actual blocker is that SIP access prerequisites were not established; provider coverage was not tested. Entitlement and permission may depend on subscription, licensing, plan tier, permitted-use restrictions, or cost. Combining these prerequisites avoids an incomplete or duplicated branch while remaining narrower than the completed general SIP Feed Investigation Planning checkpoint.

The selected pathway preserves the current IEX zero-missing-slot contract and is the least irreversible option supported by the evidence. It does not imply that the prerequisite assessment will succeed. Alternate-provider, contract-replacement, and prior-baseline pathways remain available if the prerequisite branch proves infeasible.
