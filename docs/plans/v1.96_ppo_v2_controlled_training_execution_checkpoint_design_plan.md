## Future Execution Checkpoint Objective

A later checkpoint may define a one-time controlled PPO v2 training execution procedure.

That future checkpoint must be separate from v1.96.

A future execution checkpoint may authorize only one controlled execution attempt if explicitly approved by a sealed checkpoint.

A future execution checkpoint must not authorize promotion, paper trading, live trading, controlled submit, or hybrid continuation.
