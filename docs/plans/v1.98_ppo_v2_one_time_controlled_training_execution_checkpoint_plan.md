## Planning Boundary

This checkpoint plans the structure of a future one-time controlled PPO v2 training execution checkpoint.

The future checkpoint must not be treated as model promotion.

The future checkpoint must not be treated as paper trading authorization.

The future checkpoint must not be treated as live trading authorization.

The future checkpoint must not be treated as controlled-submit authorization.

The future checkpoint must not be treated as PPO + RF or PPO + XGBoost authorization.

## Required Historical Validation and Leakage Controls

A later checkpoint must explicitly preserve:

```txt
Alpaca historical data loader as controlled historical data source
embargo gap between train and evaluation periods
embargo compliance review
train-only normalization statistics
evaluation with locked train-only normalization statistics
untouched final holdout until final validation

no candidate promotion before holdout validation and audit review
no hybrid-gate discussion before PPO-only baseline performance package
no controlled-submit discussion before candidate stability review

no controlled-submit discussion before no-submit paper observation review
```

These controls are non-optional.
